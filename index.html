About

Mallorcaddies was created to offer something Mallorca did not have — a dedicated caddie service built around the island's courses and the guests who visit them.

Based at Club de Golf Alcanada, we know the course well and take care of the people who play it.

Our guests include international golf travelers from the United States, China, Europe, and across Asia, as well as private groups, yacht and villa guests, and global travel and concierge partners.

Every group gets the same attention, whether it is a solo traveller or a group arranged through a concierge partner.
