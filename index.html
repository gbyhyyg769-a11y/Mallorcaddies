About the Company

Mallorca’s Only Dedicated Caddie Service

Mallorcaddies was created to bring the timeless tradition of caddie service to one of Mallorca’s most memorable golfing settings.

Based at Club de Golf Alcanada, we combine deep course knowledge with the warmth and ease of Mediterranean hospitality.

Our guests include international golf travelers from the United States, China, Europe, and across Asia, as well as private groups, yacht and villa guests, and global travel and concierge partners.

Every round is supported with care, local insight, and personal attention.
