About

Built around the courses. Built around the guest.

Mallorcaddies was created to fill a gap. Mallorca has some of the finest golf courses in the Mediterranean, and until now no dedicated caddie service to match.

We are based at Club de Golf Alcanada, one of the island's signature courses, and we know it well. We are also available for bookings at the Arabella Golf courses — Son Muntaner, Son Vida, and Son Quint.

Every booking is handled personally. Our co-founder and chief caddie, David manages all on-course operations. I handle everything else. Between the two of us, nothing falls through.

David runs every round. I handle every booking. You will always know exactly who you are dealing with and how to reach us.
