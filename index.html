About the Company

The Only Dedicated Caddie Service in Mallorca

Mallorcaddies was created to bring the timeless tradition of elite caddie service to Mallorca's most prestigious golfing experience. Exclusively serving Club de Golf Alcanada, we combine professional caddie expertise with the warmth, elegance, and ease of Mediterranean hospitality. Our clients include luxury golf travelers from the United States, China, Europe, and Asia, private high-net-worth groups, yacht and villa guests, and international corporate hospitality partners. Every round is delivered with discretion, precision, and personal attention.
